Ausum Person of the day

Stephen Wiltshire

Artist who draws whole cities

1

New York 2009

He drew the whole city from memory after one 20-minute helicopter ride.

2

Tokyo 2005

A drawing over ten metres long, finished in seven days after a helicopter ride.

3

A royal honour 2006

He received an MBE for services to art, and opened his gallery in London.

Did you know?

As a young child, Stephen did not talk. His first word was “paper”.

Stephen was diagnosed with autism when he was three. Drawing became his way to share what he sees.
