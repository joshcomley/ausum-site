TempleGrandin

Animal scientist and inventor

What she did
  1. At 18 she built a hug machine that squeezes gently to help her feel calm.
  2. She designed curved paths that help cattle stay calm and safe.
  3. She thinks in pictures, not words, and wrote the book Thinking in Pictures.

General noteTemple is autistic, and she talks about it openly so other people can understand.

Did you know?A shadow or a hanging chain can scare a cow. Temple was one of the first scientists to say so.
