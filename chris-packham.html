Ausum Person of the day Today

Chris Packham

Naturalist, photographer, TV presenter

Field notes

  1. 1986

    Starts presenting The Really Wild Show, a BBC nature programme for children.

  2. 2009

    Joins Springwatch, a BBC show that watches wild birds and animals.

  3. 2019

    Is given a CBE, a special honour, for helping to protect nature.

Did you know?

In 2018, Chris helped scientists rebuild a T. rex, starting from its bones, to show how it may really have looked from its bones.

Chris was diagnosed with Asperger syndrome, a kind of autism, in 2005. He talks about it openly.
